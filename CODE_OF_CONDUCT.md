# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainers through GitHub (private vulnerability reporting for security, or a maintainer contact on the repository for conduct). Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as they can.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
